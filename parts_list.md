## Discovery tokens (one draw-bag per biome)

Round tokens: **discipline icon** (💎 geo · 🐾 zoo · 🌿 bot · 🏺 arch) on a **colour** background (🟪 purple · ⬜ grey · 🟦 navy), plus **tile-event tokens** mixed in. Reverse side = the biome's back-colour (drawn face-down onto tiles).

| Bag | Specimen tokens | Event tokens | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grassland | 16 (even mix) | 2 | 18 |
| Jungle | 44 (🌿🐾-heavy) | 5 | 49 |
| Rocky | 28 (🏺 + scarce 💎) | 3 | 31 |
| Ruins | 48 (🏺 motherlode) | 6 | 54 |
| Water | 32 (🐾-heavy aquatic) | 4 | 36 |
| **Total** | **168** | **20** | **188** |

Colour split per bag: ~35% the biome's signature colour (jungle purple · rocky/water grey · ruins navy · grassland unbiased), the rest uniform.

**Tile-event tokens (20):** ⛏ rockslide, 🐗 animal attack, 🏴 bushthieves, 🧭 helpful native — weighted 2:2:1:2, with a +3 lean per biome (rocky→rockslide, jungle→animal attack, ruins→bushthieves).
