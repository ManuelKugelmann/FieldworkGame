# Expedition: Verdant Prime — Player Manual

*How to play, for players. For design intent see [`game_design.md`](./game_design.md); for a physical component list see [`parts_list.md`](./parts_list.md).*

---

## 1. The goal

You lead a scientific expedition into a monsoon-threatened wilderness. Explore the map, **catalogue specimens** (💎 geology · 🐾 zoology · 🌿 botany · 🏺 archaeology), and **publish research** before the storm ends the season. The player with the most **victory points** wins:

> **Score = prestige 🎓 + 1 point per 50k$ banked**

Research is a **race**: all discoveries you bring in end up in one shared, face-up pool, and any player may publish from it — first come, first served.

## 2. Reading the board

The map is generated fresh every match: ~200 tiles around a branching **river** (light blue channel), crossed by exactly one **road bridge** and a few **foot bridges**.

| You see | It is |
|---|---|
| 🌾 light green | **grassland** — sparse finds, even mix |
| 🌴 dark green | **jungle** — rich in 🌿 and 🐾 |
| 🪨 grey | **rocky** — 🏺 and scarce, valuable 💎 |
| 🏛️ gold-brown | **ruins** — rare dig sites, always hold a find, 🏺-rich |
| 🌊 blue channel | **river** — impassable on foot; a biome you can forage by boat |
| solid tan line | **road** — fast going, cars run here |
| dashed green line | **footpath** — fast going on foot |
| dashed blue line | **brook** — a boat-only shortcut over land |
| jagged dark band | **cliff edge** — no one crosses it, ever |
| white foam on the river | **waterfall** — boats can't pass |

**Locations** (never hold finds): 🏢 **research base** (market + research + helilift target) · ⛺ **frontier base** (research, deep in the wild) · 🏘️ **village** (market) · 🏠 **river villages** ×2 (markets on foot bridges; each starts with a canoe 🛶 and motorboat 🛥️) · 🏕️ **field camps** (research sites players deploy).

**Tokens:** a coloured disc in a tile's centre is a **face-up discovery** (icon = discipline, background = colour). A dull biome-tinted disc is an **unexplored** tile that may hold one. Discs in tile corners are **shared-pool cards** at a research site; small squares/🛶 on tile edges are **dropped gear/boats**.

Each player is a **specialist** — botanist, zoologist, geologist, or archaeologist — with a permanent **+3** when cataloguing their own discipline. Your pawn colour matches your discipline.

## 3. Your turn

You get **4 AP** per turn (in the very first round, later seats get a little more to offset going last). At the start of each round one **global event** is drawn that affects everyone (see §8). Spend AP on any mix of actions, then end your turn.

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Step to an adjacent tile (foot) | **0.5 AP** along a road/path edge, **1 AP** otherwise |
| Canoe step (carrying 🛶) | **0.25 AP** on river/brook, foot cost elsewhere |
| Boat run 🛶 / drive 🚗 🛥️ | **1 AP per 4 tiles** along river / road |
| Catalogue a find | **1 AP** + dice roll |
| Helilift 🚁 to base | **1 AP** + 120k$ (shortfall → −prestige) |
| Publish | **free**, max once per turn, at a research site |
| Buy gear, board (10k$)/leave a vehicle, drop/pick up items, deposit a specimen, deploy your camp | **free** |

## 4. Moving around

- **On foot** the river is a hard wall — cross at bridges only. Cliffs block everyone. One exception: you may wade a single step onto a water tile to fetch a **beached boat** (a dropped canoe or moored motorboat).
- **Canoe 🛶** — a carried item. While you hold it you may enter water and glide along the river and brooks at 0.25 AP/step, or make fast **boat runs** (4 river tiles per AP). Boats pass freely under bridges. Drop it on any tile for a teammate; pick it up again later. It's blocked by the waterfall and cliffs.
- **Car 🚗** — a shared vehicle on the board (one at the base, one at the village). Stand on it, pay **10k$** to board, then drive up to 4 road tiles per AP. Roads only. Stepping off is free — any foot move auto-dismounts, and the car stays where you left it.
- **Motorboat 🛥️** — like the car, but for the **river channel**: board it from an adjacent bank (10k$), drive 4 river tiles per AP, and step ashore to dock (free). One is moored at each river village.
- **Helilift 🚁** — from anywhere: 1 AP and 120k$ flies you home to the base. If you can't pay it all, the shortfall converts to **negative prestige** (40k$ ≈ 1 🎓). It's the emergency exit — expensive on your reputation.

## 5. Exploring & cataloguing

**Entering a tile flips it** (free): its discovery (if any) turns face-up, showing discipline and colour. Each tile holds **at most one** find; roads and locations hold none; ruins always deliver.

To collect a face-up find, **catalogue** it: pay **1 AP** and roll **2d6 + gear bonuses + your specialist bonus** (+3 if it's your discipline) against the colour's difficulty:

| Colour | Difficulty | Bare 2d6 odds |
|---|---|---|
| 🟪 purple | **5** | ~83% |
| ⬜ grey | **8** | ~42% |
| 🟦 navy | **13** | **0% — impossible without bonuses** |

- **Double ones (🎲1+🎲1)** → a botched dig: the find *flees* (animals) or is *destroyed*, no matter your bonuses. This is the **only** way an attempt loses the find.
- **Meet the difficulty** → the specimen goes into your hand.
- **Any other miss** → it *stays* on the tile; try again.

⚠️ Bare navy still can't succeed — a roll without bonuses only burns AP and risks the botch. Build up gear and lean on your specialty first.

Your hand holds at most **8 specimens**. A full hand blocks cataloguing — publish or deposit to make room. Opponents see *which disciplines* you carry, but not the colours.

## 6. Watch your step — tile events

Some tiles hide an event instead of (or besides) a find; it fires the moment you enter:

- ⛏ **Rockslide** — the tile's find is buried, the whole tile is sealed off for the rest of the game, and you're bumped back where you came from.
- 🐗 **Animal attack** — lose one carried thing (a specimen if you have one, else gear, else 1 AP).
- 🏴 **Bushthieves** — robbed of 30k$.
- 🧭 **Helpful native** — gain a free easy specimen of the local discipline.

## 7. Research & publishing

The 🏢 base, ⛺ frontier base, and every deployed 🏕️ field camp are **research sites**. They all serve **one shared, face-up pool** of specimen cards.

**Publishing** (free action, once per turn, at a research site):
1. Your entire hand is **dumped into the shared pool** — no holding back.
2. Assemble one **project** from the pool — *anyone's* cards are fair game.
3. The cards used are consumed into your public record; you score the project's **prestige 🎓 + money**.

Whatever you don't use **stays in the pool** for everyone — publishing feeds your rivals, so timing matters. Cheap combos score quickly; holding out for a big set risks someone else using your cards first.

**Projects** form a fixed table of poker-style hands (discipline = rank, colour = suit): pairs, triples, two-pair, full house, four-of-a-kind, colour flush, straights, and pinned-colour versions of each. Payouts scale with **colour difficulty** (navy ≫ grey ≫ purple), **discipline scarcity** (💎🌿 > 🐾 > 🏺), and **combo size**. The side panel shows what you can publish *right now* and what you're closest to.

Also at research sites: **deposit** a single specimen into the pool (free) to clear hand space, and — anywhere on empty land — **deploy your field camp** (once per game, free) to found a forward research site usable by *everyone*. Deep-wilderness publishing without the trek home.

## 8. Markets, gear & money

Money is displayed in **k$**. Earn it from publishing and events; spend it at the three market types (🏢 base, 🏘️ village, 🏠 river villages), all free actions:

| Item | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Lab kit 🔬 +1 | 30k$ | +1 to **every** catalogue roll |
| Lab kit 🔬 +2 | 60k$ | +2 to every roll |
| Lab kit 🔬 +3 | 100k$ | +3 to every roll |
| Field kit 🔬+icon | 40k$ | +3 to **one discipline** |

You carry at most **3 gear kits** (separate from your specimen hand). Gear and the canoe can be dropped on any tile and picked up by anyone — the base tile is the natural communal stash. Cars and motorboats can't be bought — they're shared fixtures of the map.

Unspent money is worth **1 point per 50k$** at the end.

## 9. The monsoon & the lab season

Each round's opening player draws one global event: usually good (🌬️ tailwind: +1 AP for everyone · 💰 +20k$ each · 🎓 +30k$ each · ☀️ calm), sometimes bad (⛏ a jungle tile turns rocky · 🌊 a bridge washes out — never the last one; anyone standing on it is washed ashore).

**Monsoon cards ⛈ wait at the bottom of the deck.** When they start surfacing, the season is ending — you'll see the count. After the 4th monsoon (at the end of a full round rotation), the field season closes and the **lab season** begins: every player gets **one final indoor turn**, in seat order. The frontier pool merges into the base lab, your hand is dumped in, and you may make **one last publish** from everything gathered. Then scores are tallied.

**Don't get caught deep in the wild with a full hand when the storm hits** — one lab publish is all the amnesty you get.

## 10. Quick reference

- **Turn:** 4 AP · move / catalogue (1 AP) / publish (free, 1×) / free actions · end turn.
- **Catalogue:** 2d6 + gear + specialty ≥ 5 / 8 / 13 (🟪/⬜/🟦). Miss by ≤2 = stays; worse = lost.
- **Limits:** hand 8 specimens · 3 gear kits · 1 publish per turn · 1 field camp per game.
- **Score:** prestige + banked money ÷ 50k$.

**Tips**
- Open by foraging your specialty near the base — the +3 makes even grey finds reliable.
- The canoe is the strongest mover on the map: brooks and the river are 0.25 AP highways, and the water biome itself holds finds. Race for it.
- Publish little and often *or* hold for a premium set — but remember every publish dumps your whole hand into the common pool.
- Deploy your camp at the far end of a rich region, not next to an existing base.
- Keep 120k$-worth of caution: getting sealed in by rockslides or cut off by a washout means a helilift — or a reputation hit.
